# 3D BuilderCrap 1.0.4

Select a part to see Move, Rotate and Scale at the bottom of the viewport. The colored handles have no text badges. Release a handle to commit one edit, or press Escape during the drag to cancel. In Scale, drag a corner to keep the proportions. Type an exact position, angle or dimension and press Enter to accept it. Roll, Pitch and Yaw follow the orientation of a single part through drags, Undo and reopening. The Δ° indicator identifies selections that use relative rotation.

The floor uses 10 mm squares. Measurements start at the world origin and repeat every 50 mm along positive X and Y. The Grid switch controls the floor and rulers together.

Object → Settle previews placing the selection on the bed at its existing orientation. File commands are in the upper-left menu. Save, Undo and Redo stay at the upper right, and the Items pane can collapse to give the model more room.

Selection now follows the object's visible silhouette and holes in blue. Assigned colors stay visible. Unpainted faces use a light neutral gray that also carries through the first brush stroke and default color exports. Existing assigned colors keep their saved RGB values.
